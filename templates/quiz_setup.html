How this quiz is built

  1. The concept and level go to the Concept Clarifier Agent.
  2. It searches your knowledge base for passages on that concept.
  3. It writes questions grounded in those passages, plus distractors based on mistakes you have actually made.
  4. Correct answers stay on the server until you submit.
